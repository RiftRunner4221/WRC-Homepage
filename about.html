About the Wildlife Restoration Center

Founded in 1998, the Wildlife Restoration Center (WRC) is a nonprofit organization dedicated to protecting wildlife, restoring habitats, and advancing environmental research. Our teams work alongside scientists, conservationists, educators, and volunteers to preserve ecosystems for future generations.


Our Mission

Our mission is to restore ecosystems, rehabilitate injured wildlife, and support scientific research that improves conservation efforts across North America.


Our Values

🌿 Conservation

Protecting wildlife and preserving biodiversity.

🔬 Research

Using science to better understand our environment.

🤝 Community

Working with volunteers and local organizations.
