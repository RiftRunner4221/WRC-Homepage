Conservation Projects

The Wildlife Restoration Center works across North America to restore habitats, protect endangered wildlife, and support healthy ecosystems.



🦅 Eagle Rehabilitation Initiative

Providing medical treatment and rehabilitation for injured birds of prey before returning them to the wild.

🌲 Forest Recovery Program

Restoring forests damaged by wildfire, disease, and human activity.

🐺 Wildlife Monitoring Network

Tracking wildlife populations through camera traps, field observations, and satellite data.

💧 Wetland Restoration

Rebuilding wetland ecosystems that support birds, amphibians, and aquatic life.
