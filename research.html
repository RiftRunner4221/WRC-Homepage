Research

The Wildlife Restoration Center conducts scientific research to improve conservation efforts, understand ecosystem changes, and protect wildlife populations through evidence-based practices.



🦅 Wildlife Monitoring

Tracking animal populations and migration patterns using GPS collars, camera traps, and field observations.

🌲 Habitat Restoration

Studying the long-term recovery of forests, wetlands, and grasslands after environmental damage.

🧬 Biodiversity Studies

Measuring ecosystem health through species diversity and population surveys.
